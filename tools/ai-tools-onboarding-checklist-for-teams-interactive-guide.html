📋 About this checklist
Adopting AI tools as a team requires structure, not hype. This onboarding checklist helps you move from scattered experiments to a streamlined workflow. Start by defining clear success metrics — don't just adopt AI for the sake of it. Map which data sources your team can safely connect, then choose tools that integrate with your existing stack (Slack, Notion, or Jira). Always set up a sandbox before rolling out to everyone; a pilot with 2–5 members reveals friction early. Create bite‑size training and a feedback loop. Once your team is comfortable, scale gradually.
