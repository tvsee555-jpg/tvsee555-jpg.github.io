Why your keyboard switch matters for coding

Mechanical keyboard switches define your typing experience — sound, feel, and even speed. Linear switches (like Cherry MX Red) are smooth and quiet, great for open offices. Tactile switches (like Brown or Clear) give a gentle bump, helping you avoid typos during long coding sessions. Clicky switches (like Blue or Green) provide audible feedback, but can be noisy around others.

For programmers, the best switch often balances precision and comfort. Many developers love the Cherry MX Brown (tactile, medium resistance) or the ultra-smooth Gateron Yellow (linear, budget-friendly). If you're into heavy typing, try Kailh Box White (clicky, crisp).
