Why the right switch matters for programming

Mechanical keyboard switches shape your typing feel, sound, and speed. Coders often prefer linear switches (like Cherry MX Red or Gateron Yellow) for smooth, fast keystrokes, while tactile switches (Cherry MX Brown, Kailh Box Brown) give a gentle bump to confirm each press — great for avoiding typos. Clicky switches (like Cherry MX Blue or Kailh Box Jade) produce a crisp click, satisfying for some, but can be noisy in shared spaces.
