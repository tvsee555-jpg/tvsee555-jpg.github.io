4K vs Ultrawide for coding — which one wins? For developers, the choice between 4K (3840×2160) and Ultrawide 3440×1440 depends on your workflow. 4K offers razor-sharp text, perfect for long hours with small fonts — ideal for IDEs like VS Code or IntelliJ. Ultrawide gives you two virtual windows side by side without bezels, great for simultaneous documentation, terminals, and previews.
