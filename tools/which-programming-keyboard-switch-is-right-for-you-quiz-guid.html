🔍 Mechanical switches: why it matters for coders

Your keyboard switch defines every keystroke. Tactile switches (like Cherry MX Brown) give a gentle bump — great for typists who want confirmation without noise. Linear switches (Cherry MX Red, Gateron Yellow) are smooth and fast, ideal for double-taps and gaming between coding sessions. Clicky switches (Cherry MX Blue, Kailh Box White) produce an audible click; many programmers love the sound, but open offices may not.

We recommend Cherry MX Brown (tactile, ~$6.50 per switch set) for balanced all‑day coding. For speed, try Gateron Yellow (linear, ~$3.80 per 10-pack). If you want feedback, the Kailh Box White (clicky, ~$5.20 per 10) is a favourite. Wireless enthusiasts often choose Keychron hot-swap boards with Gateron switches.
