🔍 Understanding mechanical keyboard switches

Choosing the right switch for programming is about feel, sound, and actuation force. Linear switches (like Cherry MX Red, Gateron Yellow) offer smooth keystrokes — great for rapid typing. Tactile switches (Cherry MX Brown, Kailh Box Brown) give a gentle bump without loud clicks, perfect for coders who want feedback without noise. Clicky switches (Cherry MX Blue, Razer Green) provide both bump and audible click — satisfying but loud for shared spaces.
