### 3. The Keyboard Upgrade: The Mechanical Switch Revolution

Every programmer has a keyboard. But they want a better one. Mechanical keyboards are the ultimate rabbit hole for developers, but you don’t need to go into custom keycaps and soldering. A pre-built, hot-swappable mechanical keyboard with a comfortable layout is a gift that will be used for years.

Why it’s perfect: Membrane keyboards feel mushy. Mechanical switches provide tactile feedback or a smooth linear stroke. This reduces typing errors and makes the whole experience more enjoyable. Plus, they sound satisfying (assuming they don’t trigger office drama with loud clicky switches).
