What to Consider Before Comparing Prices

Price alone isn’t the whole story. Here are the key factors that influence which tool offers the best value for your specific needs:

  • Monthly vs. annual billing: Most tools offer a 15-20% discount for annual plans.
  • Context window size: Larger context windows (e.g., 100K tokens vs. 32K) let the AI understand more of your codebase, reducing the need to re-explain project structure.
  • Supported languages and frameworks: Ensure the tool excels in your primary stack (Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Rust, etc.).
  • IDE integration depth: Full inline suggestions and chat in VS Code, JetBrains, or Neovim matter more than a basic web UI.
  • Code privacy and security: If you work with proprietary code, look for local processing options or clear data usage policies.
  • Usage caps: Many "unlimited" plans have fair-use limits. Check if they apply to you.

Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

Before you buy, consider these potential cost multipliers:

  1. Context window limitations: Tools with smaller context windows may require you to repeatedly feed context, slowing you down. Larger windows (Cursor uses 100K tokens) save time but cost more to run.

  2. Premium model quotas: Tools that separate "completions" from "premium requests" (like Cursor) force you to pay more if you rely heavily on chat-based AI assistance.

  3. Team overhead: Adding users across team plans can quickly scale. Copilot Business at $19/user/user/month for 10 devs = $190/month. Cursor Business at $40/user/user/month for 10 = $400/month.

  4. VPN/proxy compatibility: Some tools (especially Tabnine Enterprise) work better in restricted networks. Test before committing.
